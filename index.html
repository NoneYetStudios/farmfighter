🕹️ TOURNAMENT CONTROLS & COMBAT GUIDE

Easy to learn. Ridiculous to master.

⌨️ Desktop Player 1

  • Move Left / Right: A / D
  • Jump: W
  • Crouch / Guard Block: S (or hold away from attacker)
  • Normal Attack: F (Fast 3-hit combo, air kick, low sweep)
  • Crazy Attack: G (Use with Forward, Back/Down, or Up for all 3 Specials!)
  • DO NOT PRESS THIS BUTTON: H

⌨️ Desktop Player 2 (Local Versus)

  • Move Left / Right: ◀ / ▶ Arrow Keys
  • Jump: ▲ Up Arrow
  • Crouch / Guard Block: ▼ Down Arrow
  • Normal Attack: 1 (Top row or Numpad)
  • Crazy Attack: 2 (Top row or Numpad)
  • DO NOT PRESS THIS BUTTON: 3 (Top row or Numpad)

📱 Mobile & Tablet Controls

  • Virtual Joystick (Left): Drag the thumbstick on the left to walk, jump, or crouch-block.
  • Stacked Attack Buttons (Right): Top button for Normal Attack (rapid combos), bottom button for Crazy Attack (character specials).
  • Giant Red Button (Bottom): Literally labeled DO NOT PRESS THIS BUTTON. Spans across the bottom for instant chaotic spectacles.
